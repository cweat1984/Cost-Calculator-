Profit for every $1 spent on equipment

How dollar-for-dollar profit works: (Customer revenue − equipment rent) ÷ equipment rent. For example, $5.40 means you keep $5.40 in margin after equipment rent for every $1 you pay to rent it. It is not net profit after all business expenses.

Assumptions: The original deal is $800/day charged and $875/week paid for each unit. Rates apply equally to every unit. Fixed 52-week rental commitment; revenue uses the billable weeks entered. Margin shown is revenue less equipment rent only, excluding labor, transport, taxes, repairs, insurance, fuel, finance charges, and other expenses. A lease can create an obligation even if customer billing stops.
